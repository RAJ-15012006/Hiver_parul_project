3. ESCALATION TRIAGE: Employs a hybrid decision engine where deterministic rules
   catch legal/fraud threats immediately, and an LLM evaluates high-stakes intents
   (Account Access, Billing) to decide whether to AUTO-HANDLE or ESCALATE to a human
   specialist with an explicit stated rationale.

To prove the system works, I constructed a 200-sample hand-calibrated Golden
Evaluation Set, benchmarked against 3 ML baselines (Majority Class, TF-IDF + LogReg,
and TF-IDF + LinearSVC), built a 5-dimension LLM Judge, proved through an ablation

Streamlit web application at hiver-parul.streamlit.app."

          |
          v

    - Injects hard negative constraints into the prompt
          |

    - all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Embeddings (384-dim, L2-normalized)

    - Sanitizes historical monetary promises ($5 credits, gift cards)
          |
          v
[ 4. Draft Reply Synthesis ]
    - Constrained by extracted slots and sanitized past replies
    - Twitter Brevity Guard: Strictly <= 240 chars with agent sign-off (^RG)
          |
          v
[ 5. Escalation Triage Engine ]
    - Deterministic Hard Rules: Legal threats, regulatory, account security
    - High-Stakes Intent Routing (ACCOUNT_ACCESS, BILLING_CHARGE)
    - Output: { AUTO | ESCALATE, Stated Rationale }

1. eda.py & notebooks/eda.ipynb (Exploratory Data Analysis)

   - Key Insight: Customer tweets average 104.7 chars; brand replies average 108.8

     builds an exact, persistent FAISS search index.

   - Key Details: Uses IndexFlatIP with L2-normalized vectors. Sets USE_TF=0 and
     TRANSFORMERS_NO_TF=1 to avoid TensorFlow import deadlocks on macOS.

   - Purpose: Defines the 8 business intents, operational descriptions, and
     high-precision regex keyword lists for deterministic fallback.
   - Tools: Python dataclasses, re.
   - Taxonomy: ORDER_STATUS, REFUND_RETURN, PRODUCT_ISSUE, ACCOUNT_ACCESS,
     DELIVERY_PROBLEM, BILLING_CHARGE, PRIME_MEMBERSHIP, GENERAL_INQUIRY.

4. src/agent.py (Core AI Agent Engine)
   - Purpose: The central orchestrator. Implements classify(), extract_slots(),
     sanitize_retrieved_reply(), draft_reply(), decide_escalation(), and run_agent().
   - Tools: groq Python SDK, re.
   - Key Innovation: Slot-Guardrail prevents Order ID Fixation by injecting
     negative instructions if a Tracking ID is already supplied or if the issue
     is account-related.

5. src/baselines.py (Classical ML Benchmarks)
   - Purpose: Trains and evaluates 3 classical ML algorithms on the Golden Set:
     (1) MajorityClassBaseline (DummyClassifier)
     (2) TFIDFLogRegBaseline (Character n-grams 2-5 + LogisticRegression)
     (3) TFIDFSVMBaseline (Word n-grams 1-3 + LinearSVC)
   - Tools: scikit-learn (TfidfVectorizer, LogisticRegression, LinearSVC, Pipeline).

6. src/llm_judge.py (Automated Quality Auditor)
   - Purpose: Evaluates generated replies on a 1-5 scale across 5 dimensions:
     Helpfulness, Empathy, Accuracy, Conciseness, and Brand Voice.
   - Tools: groq SDK (qwen/qwen3.8-27b or llama-3.3-70b-versatile).

4. CORE MACHINE LEARNING & NLP THEORY (INTERVIEW QUESTIONS)
================================================================================

Q1: Why use FAISS Flat Inner Product (IP) instead of HNSW or IVF?
Answer:
"For 23,661 vectors of 384 dimensions, the raw index is only 35MB. An exact flat
search takes under 15ms on a standard CPU. HNSW and IVF are approximate nearest
neighbor (ANN) algorithms designed for millions of vectors. Using ANN here would
introduce quantization/approximation recall loss with zero perceptible latency
benefit. Flat IP guarantees 100% recall with mathematically exact cosine similarity."

Q2: Why does Inner Product (IP) equal Cosine Similarity in your pipeline?
Answer:
"Cosine similarity is dot product divided by the product of vector magnitudes:
(A . B) / (||A|| * ||B||). In src/embeddings.py, we apply faiss.normalize_L2(embeddings),
which scales every vector so ||A|| = 1. Therefore, the denominator is 1 * 1 = 1,
making the dot product (Inner Product) mathematically identical to Cosine Similarity,
but computed significantly faster."

Q3: Bi-Encoder vs. Cross-Encoder: Why all-MiniLM-L6-v2?
Answer:
"A Bi-encoder encodes sentences independently into dense vectors. This allows us
to precompute the 23,661 historical resolutions into a static FAISS index once.
At inference, we only encode the incoming tweet (1 forward pass) and perform a vector
search in milliseconds. A Cross-encoder passes both query and candidate together
through attention layers; running that across 23,661 pairs would require 23,661
forward passes per incoming tweet, taking 30+ seconds. Bi-encoders are the industry
standard for scalable RAG retrieval."

Q4: Why are BLEU and ROUGE misleading for conversational customer support?
Answer:
"BLEU measures n-gram precision; ROUGE measures n-gram recall against historical
reference tweets. In customer support, two completely different sentences can have
100% operational equivalence. For example:
Reference: 'Please direct message us your order ID.'
Agent:     'Kindly DM us your order number so we can help.'
BLEU gives this almost a 0.0 score because exact word tokens differ, even though
the reply is practically perfect. BLEU penalizes valid paraphrasing. That is why
an LLM-as-a-Judge with an explicit rubric is a far more reliable quality signal."

Q5: Macro F1 vs. Weighted F1: Why did you prioritize Macro F1?
Answer:
"Macro F1 calculates the F1 score for each class independently and computes their
unweighted average. Weighted F1 weights each score by class frequency. In Twitter
support, routine inquiries (ORDER_STATUS) represent 31% of the data, while
GENERAL_INQUIRY is only 4%. If an agent fails on rare classes, Weighted F1 remains
falsely high, whereas Macro F1 severely penalizes per-class failure. Macro F1
proves our agent generalizes across all 8 business classes."

Q6: What is Pearson vs. Spearman correlation in your evaluation?
Answer:
"Pearson correlation (r) measures linear correlation between continuous variables.
Spearman correlation (rho) measures monotonic rank correlation. In our evaluation,
human baseline scores on past Amazon human agents clustered around 4.3-4.7, while
the LLM judge scored generated replies (mean 3.15). The divergence revealed that
the judge actively caught subtle AI hallucinations (like inventing gift card refunds)
that human evaluators never encountered in the ground truth."

8. THE 5 CORE FAILURE MODES & HOW GUARDRAILS SOLVED THEM
================================================================================

Mode 1: Order ID Fixation on Non-Order Queries (Account lockout, general inquiry).
  - Hypothesis: Historical training data contains "DM us your order ID" in >65%
    of replies, creating a high-probability conversational shortcut.
  - Fix: Entity Slot-Guardrail injects negative constraints ("Account issue. Do
    NOT ask for Order ID. Direct to secure link: [URL]"). Fixation dropped from
    37.5% to 0.0%.

Mode 2: Multi-Issue Semantic Boundary Bleed (Compound Inquiries).
  - Hypothesis: Single-label softmax collapses compound statements ("item damaged,
    want refund") onto the refund keyword, missing the defect.
  - Fix: Disambiguation hierarchy in CLASSIFY_SYSTEM prioritizes physical damage
    (PRODUCT_ISSUE) over general returns, jumping PRODUCT_ISSUE F1 from 0.00 to 0.86.

Mode 3: Hallucinated Resolution Artifacts via RAG Context Leakage.
  - Hypothesis: Retrieved historical tweets contain one-off discretionary credits
    ($5 credits, gift cards) that the LLM repeats as universal promises.
  - Fix: Pre-prompt regex sanitization neutralizes monetary promises from RAG
    context before prompt injection. Leakage dropped to 0.0%.

Mode 4: False Sense of Resolution on Missing/Stolen Deliveries.
  - Hypothesis: "Marked delivered but missing" is misdiagnosed as transit delay
    (ORDER_STATUS) rather than package theft (DELIVERY_PROBLEM).
  - Fix: Explicit regex routing sends "delivered but missing/stolen" to
    DELIVERY_PROBLEM for carrier investigation and neighbor check steps.

Mode 5: Ambiguous Policy vs. Cancellation Intent in Prime Membership.
  - Hypothesis: Subscription fee inquiries ("Why was Prime $14.99?") trigger
    unnecessary human escalation under BILLING_CHARGE fraud heuristics.
  - Fix: Subscription keyword filter separates scheduled Prime fees from
    unauthorized card fraud, preserving human tier-2 bandwidth.
